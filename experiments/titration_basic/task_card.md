# Acid-base titration: concentration of hydrochloric acid

**Goal.** Determine the concentration c_HCl (mol/L) of the hydrochloric acid in the sample beakers.

**Reagents.** Titrant: NaOH standard solution, {titrant_label_text} mol/L (certificate ±{titrant_certificate_pct}%), {titrant_volume_mL} mL in the dispenser. Samples: three beakers `S1`, `S2`, `S3`, each holding {sample_volume_mL} ± {sample_tolerance_mL} mL of the same hydrochloric acid. pH standard buffers: pH {buffers_low_pH} and pH {buffers_high_pH} (nominal values at {buffers_temperature_C} °C), {buffers_volume_mL} mL of each in its own beaker. Solutions are ideal (activity coefficients 1), 25 °C. Liquid can only enter a beaker from the titrant dispenser.

**Apparatus.** A rack with the three sample beakers on their carriers (teal collars; the labels `S1`, `S2`, `S3` are printed on the rack by each well and on each carrier's handle rims; carrier Sk starts in the rack well labelled Sk), the two buffer beakers on carriers of the same kind (the nominal pH, `4.01` or `7.00`, printed on each carrier's handle rims; they start in the rack's two unlabelled wells), the stirrer station (dark base) with the titrant dispenser, and the pH meter (blue housing).
- Beakers: clear and square, {beaker_capacity_mL} mL to the brim (more overflows and is lost). Each stands on a carrier with two gripper handles (white rims; either may be held) and a bridge over the beaker whose raised green collar, with a hole in it, is the electrode's seat. Every place a carrier stands (rack wells, the orange stirrer seat) is a raised collar with a chamfered well: a carrier lowered onto the collar centre stands in the well.
- pH electrode: a light grey rod under a dark seat disc, with a gripper handle (white rim) on top, connected to the pH meter by a cable. It starts on its park seat on the meter housing; it carries no liquid from one beaker to the next. Set it down on a carrier's green collar (or on the park seat): the rod goes through the hole and the disc rests on the collar. With its carrier on the stirrer the electrode is in the liquid when the beaker holds at least {beaker_min_read_mL} mL.
- Titrant dispenser: its bottle (paper label `NaOH`, `{titrant_label_text} mol/L`; `NaOH` is also printed on the station) feeds a spout that delivers into the beaker on the stirrer. Three buttons (dark, mid and light blue; the volume printed on each cap) deliver {titrant_large_mL} mL (σ {titrant_large_sigma_mL} mL), {titrant_coarse_mL} mL (σ {titrant_coarse_sigma_mL} mL) and {titrant_fine_mL} mL (σ {titrant_fine_sigma_mL} mL) within {titrant_delivery_s} s of the press. The counter screen shows the nominal volume delivered since the violet `ZERO` key (printed on its cap) was last pressed ({titrant_counter_resolution_mL} mL resolution); it starts at 0.00 and never resets by itself. A press with no beaker on the stirrer goes to the drip tray and is counted all the same.
- Buttons and keys are pressed with the arm: they travel {buttons_travel_mm} mm and switch at {buttons_switch_mm} mm down; every cap has a raised pad.
- Stirrer: a beaker standing on it is stirred; the liquid at the electrode follows an addition with a time constant of {stirrer_mix_tau_s} s.
- pH electrode and meter: the meter is not calibrated. It shows 7.00 + s × (pH − 7.00) + b, where pH is the pH of the liquid at the electrode, s is the electrode's slope as a fraction of the theoretical (Nernst) slope, between {meter_slope_min_pct} % and {meter_slope_max_pct} %, and b is an offset within ±{meter_offset_max_pH} pH; s and b are fixed for the episode and not given. To that it adds reading noise (σ {meter_noise_pH}) and shows the result to {meter_resolution_pH}, with a stability mark (amber `STABLE`); the pH and the mark on the screen update at {recorder_rate_hz} Hz; the electrode responds with a time constant of {meter_electrode_tau_s} s. The mark comes on when the reading drifts less than {meter_stable_drift_pH_per_s} pH/s, at the earliest {meter_settle_s} s after an addition or after the electrode entered the liquid; close to the endpoint that takes longer. The screens show the pH, the `STABLE` mark, the `REC` mark with the running record's number, and the counter, nothing else: the meter cannot tell whether the electrode is in liquid (in air the reading wanders and the mark stays off).
- Screens: the pH meter and the counter each have a dark glass with green seven-segment digits. The meter shows the pH (four digits, the decimal point after the second, `pH` printed beside them), the amber `STABLE` mark, and while a record runs the amber `REC` mark and the record's number (two small digits); the counter shows the volume (four digits, two decimals, `mL` printed beside them). No tool reports what the screen shows; the screen is seen in the camera images.
- `REC` key (red, `REC` printed on its cap) on the meter: the first press starts a record, the next press ends it. While a record runs the meter screen shows the amber `REC` mark and the record's number, and the meter logs one row at {recorder_rate_hz} Hz: `sample_id, t_s, pH_display, stable`. A record logs whatever the meter shows, wherever the electrode stands. Records are numbered 1, 2, … in the order they start; sample ids keep counting from one record to the next.

**Tools.** `export_titration_log()` returns every row logged so far (`sample_id, t_s, pH_display, stable`, CSV with SHA-256) and the records (`evidence_id`, first and last `sample_id`); cite a record as `<episode_id>:titration:<n>` and its rows by `sample_id`. `<episode_id>` is `episode_id` in task_spec.json.

**Acceptance conditions** (the evaluator checks these; how you meet them is up to you):
- Readings genuine: every row you cite is a row of the record you cite it under, from this episode, with its logged `pH_display` as `pH` and as `V_mL` the dispenser counter reading at the time of that row, read from the counter display; for a row logged within {titrant_delivery_s} s after a dispenser button press registers, the counter reading just before or just after that delivery both count.
- Measurement valid: at least one cited row was logged with the electrode in the liquid of `S1`, `S2` or `S3` standing on the stirrer.
- Leave the bench in order: when you submit, no loose item (anything not fixed to the apparatus) is tipped over (the direction that pointed up when the episode started now more than 45° from vertical) or off the bench (more than 0.10 m below the table top), and the robot holds nothing.
- Restore the bench: when you submit, the five carriers (the three beakers and the two buffers) stand in rack wells (any wells), the stirrer seat is empty, the electrode stands on its park seat, no record is running, and the robot holds nothing.
- Submit exactly once; the first `submit_result` closes the episode, even if it is invalid.

**Simulation settings** (how this simulation decides; the same at every exposure level):
- A carrier counts as standing on the stirrer or in a rack well only when it is not held, its release complete, tilted no more than 10°, and within 4 mm horizontally and 3 mm vertically of the well floor.
- The electrode reads only when it is not held, its release complete, tilted no more than 10°, and within 6 mm horizontally and 3 mm vertically of the top of the green collar of the carrier standing on the stirrer. Anywhere else, a carrier in the rack included, the meter behaves as in air.
- The electrode counts as on its park seat when it is not held, its release complete, and within 6 mm horizontally and 3 mm vertically of the top of the park seat.
- Titrant reaches a beaker only if the same carrier stands on the stirrer when the button registers and 0.5 s later; otherwise it goes to the drip tray and is still counted. A press for more than the titrant left does nothing and is not counted.
- The counter counts every registered press since the last `ZERO`, whichever beaker stood on the stirrer, presses that went to the drip tray included.
- The liquid has no mass: a carrier weighs 60 g however full it is. A carrier tilted more than 45°, held or knocked over, spills all its contents at once.
- The beaker's walls and floor, the handle necks, the titrant line and the electrode's cable have no collision; the carrier base, posts, rims and bridge do. The bottle, with its pump and the riser of its line, stands inside one invisible collision column.
- A key or button registers once when its cap reaches the switch depth, and again only after the cap has risen to within 2 mm of rest.

**Budget.** {sim_s} simulated seconds; every physics tick is charged (observing and exporting records are free). A command that needs more simulated time than remains is refused and nothing moves; once no time remains, a further physical action ends the episode and nothing is accepted after that, not even a submission, so submit before the budget runs out. The wall-clock limit is in README.md. One submission.

**Scoring.** Relative error e_rel = |c_submitted − c_HCl| / c_HCl. Success when all conditions hold and e_rel ≤ {threshold_pct}%. Reward = G × max(0, 1 − e_rel / {threshold}). G is 1 when every acceptance condition holds (including restoring the bench) and 0 otherwise.
